
# Marketing Analytics Dashboard (Revenue, Sales & Profit Analysis)

This projects focuses on analyzing business performance data including revenue, sales, and profit using Excel and Power BI. The goal is to extract meaningful insights that can support marketing strategies, pricing decisions, and business growth.

Objectives:

Analyze revenue, sales, and profit trends
Identify high-performing and low-performing products
Track monthly and overall business performance
Support data-driven decision-making for marketing and sales

Tools & Technologies:

Microsoft Excel
Power BI

Project Files:

Sales_Profit_Dashboard.pbix
→ Interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing sales and profit performance
Gross_Revenue_Dashboard.xlsx
→ Excel dashboard for tracking revenue trends and business metrics
dashboard-screenshots/
→ Contains visual previews of dashboards for quick understanding

## Features


Key Features:

Interactive dashboards for revenue, sales, and profit analysis
